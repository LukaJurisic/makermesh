# Public All Gas builds: quick competitive read

Checked September 19, 2026. This is a small primary-source sample, not a leaderboard or an assessment of who will win. A public listing or builder claim does not establish accepted judging eligibility. Competitor integrations were not exercised; README statements are identified as claims unless source code was inspected.

The main research pass directly observed **40 tagged apps** on the [public All Gas gallery](https://vibeapps.dev/tag/allgashackathon). The judging gallery requires an access code; public tagging is distinct from acceptance into judging.

## Closest public listing comparisons

The main research pass also opened these first-party VibeApps detail pages directly:

- [Quote Arena](https://vibeapps.dev/s/quote-arena), dated September 18: home-repair requests, contractor sourcing, email replies and quote comparison, with a concrete numerical example of a revised reply changing the order. It provides app, source, video and social links.
- [Compari](https://vibeapps.dev/s/compari), dated September 18: clarifies a buyer request, finds vendors, filters and contacts approved choices. The builder explicitly identifies obtaining vendor replies as difficult and needing follow-up. Lesson: a sourcing product must account for real response delays; do not promise immediate complete quotes.
- [Ombuds](https://vibeapps.dev/s/ombuds), dated September 18: care-home records and availability enquiries. Its author describes owned-inbox simulated personas, a follow-up for a missing price, provenance export, five fresh-session tests and an approximately two-minute full flow. Its reported $0.08–$0.11 cost per run and completion time were not independently measured. Lesson: an interactive complete example can be more compelling than separate screens. MakerMesh must clearly explain the boundary between custom research and the captured Atlas exchange.

## What MakerMesh should learn today

1. **Lead with the purchase decision that changes.** Quote Arena's README illustrates a cheaper but incomplete quote losing until its timing is clarified. MakerMesh's strongest concrete sequence is the captured French reply becoming an inspectable quote and requirement evidence. Show what it resolves and what remains unknown. Keep Atlas visibly fictional and distinguish the captured exchange from new live research.
2. **Differentiate beyond the sponsor stack.** Quote Arena already describes a discovery-to-email-to-comparison loop. MakerMesh's ceramics specialization, bilingual correspondence, explicit buyer requirements and source-to-claim traceability should carry the story. This is positioning advice, not proof of uniqueness across all entrants.
3. **Make proof easy to reach.** The sampled projects link a usable example, short walkthrough, source and build record. MakerMesh already has a no-account example; make those submission links public and mutually consistent after the publication gate is resolved.
4. **Explain one meaningful limitation beside the benefit.** Fillable's supply-versus-stock distinction and ThesisLine's quotation-versus-interpretation distinction strengthen comprehension. MakerMesh should be equally clear that a public sourcing lead is not a qualified supplier, and that an EXW quote excluding shipping is not a delivered Toronto price.
5. **Get a real target user's reaction.** Ask a cafe owner or buyer which missing term would prevent ordering a sample. A specific observation and resulting fix would strengthen the submission more than adding unrelated features. Do not imply user validation, saved hours or conversion results before measuring them.

## Do not copy blindly

- Quote Arena's code applies explicit penalties for missing fields and assumes a 21-day timeline when absent. MakerMesh should retain its own rule that unknowns do not earn points, and must not publish negative rankings of identifiable real suppliers. The lesson is explainability, not adopting their formula.
- Source citations, safety language and test counts appear across multiple entries. They support credibility but should not replace a memorable product outcome in the opening pitch.
- No reliable comparative social engagement conclusion was established. Search snippets, follower totals and mutable like counts do not establish traction or judging advantage.

## Search limitations

GitHub and the linked first-party Fillable post were directly readable. Several other LinkedIn pages returned 429/999, and social mirrors were used only to discover original links. RegVista and NoticeProof appeared in indexed LinkedIn activity, but are excluded from the concrete sample because direct original-post/repository verification was incomplete. This note does not treat unrelated past hackathon winners as current entries.
