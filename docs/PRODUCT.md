# MakerMesh product scope

## Mission

Describe what you need made. MakerMesh discovers relevant makers, determines what the
web cannot answer, obtains structured responses with buyer approval, and creates an
evidence-backed supplier network around the request.

## Frozen wedge

Custom Moroccan ceramics for cafés, boutique hotels, interior designers, and independent
retailers in Canada, the United Kingdom, and Europe.

The controlled project is 200 handcrafted eight-ounce espresso cups for the fictional
Harbour Coffee Lab in Toronto. Atlas Clay Studio is a fictional demonstration supplier.

## Core loop

1. A buyer approves a structured brief.
2. Firecrawl discovers public supplier footprints and durable source pages.
3. OpenAI extracts claims with exact evidence references.
4. Deterministic rules surface confirmed, contradicted, unknown, and not-applicable fields.
5. OpenAI turns unresolved keys into editable, focused questions.
6. The buyer approves a controlled AgentMail message.
7. A normal email reply is preserved and structured.
8. Deterministic comparison updates reactively.
9. A consent-aware Mesh Passport preview assembles confirmed claims.

## Required public journeys

- Understand the premise and open the demo without signing in.
- Compose, edit, and approve the espresso-cup brief.
- Inspect captured-live research progress and every evidence source.
- See question gaps without unknown values becoming successes.
- Review English and French outreach in a consequential approval drawer.
- Inspect the original fictional reply beside structured facts.
- Compare eligibility, preferences, evidence, and commercial completeness separately.
- Open a premium, explicitly fictional Mesh Passport preview.

## Reputation boundary

Real suppliers may appear with public names, locations, official websites, attributed
excerpts, observed capabilities, and explicit unknowns. Public views never grade, reject,
negatively rank, quote, or publish passports for identifiable real businesses. Full
qualification and comparison behavior is demonstrated only with fictional entities.

## Out of scope

Payments, escrow, purchasing, freight, customs, duties, tax, lending, insurance,
WhatsApp, calls, autonomous negotiation, follow-ups, reviews, star ratings, broad country
or category coverage, native mobile apps, supplier onboarding, and general chat.
