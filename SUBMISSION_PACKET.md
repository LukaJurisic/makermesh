## Product description

Small businesses often know what they need made but lack a structured way to find and
compare suitable workshops. MakerMesh starts with that request: it structures the brief,
researches public supplier footprints, preserves evidence, and identifies the questions
the web cannot answer. Buyer-approved email replies become comparable capability claims.

The demonstration follows a fictional Toronto café sourcing 200 custom Moroccan ceramic
espresso cups. AI extracts information; deterministic rules evaluate requirements.
Unknowns remain unknown, original currencies and quote bases stay visible, and public
supplier facts are separated from fictional demonstration grades and quotes.

## Stack and evidence

React, Vite, and TypeScript frontend on Convex static hosting. Convex runs the database,
functions, realtime subscriptions, auth, workflow, rate limits, and integration components.
OpenAI has compiled the controlled brief; Firecrawl has performed real search and a
five-page durable crawl. Captured research proof is visible in the public demo.

AgentMail delivered the approved RFQ and received the separately approved French reply on

was verified. The reviewed backend/frontend is deployed to the development site. The
sanitized reply result is published with explicit approval and verified in the public
demo. It is separated from fixture inbox activity and the fictional market.

## Current custom research

Visitors can create a Moroccan-ceramics request, review the AI-structured brief, approve
bounded bilingual research, and inspect exact source statements plus confirmation questions.
Convex owns the durable workflow, browser-private results, expiry, and atomic usage limits.
Real sourcing leads are not scored or represented as verified manufacturers. The captured
fictional Atlas exchange separately demonstrates the approved email-to-comparison loop.
